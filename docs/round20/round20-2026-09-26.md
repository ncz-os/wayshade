# Round 20 — six new xshadertoy-format original shaders

**Date:** 2026-09-26

Six new original GLSL fragment shaders for the xshadertoy-format
catalogue. All single-pass, all GLSL ES 3.00, all original work
(MIT), all registered in `meson.build` and built into standalone
executables alongside the existing 38 vendored shaders.

## Algorithm families covered

| # | Shader | Algorithm | Brief reference |
|--:|--------|-----------|-----------------|
| 1 | `leviathan` | Three-movement volumetric flythrough (raymarched interior + polar coordinate corridor + heightfield terrain) | Priority per operator request |
| 2 | `physarum` | Doubly domain-warped fbm with curl-noise advection, thresholded into filament network | "Physarum / slime-mould transport network" |
| 3 | `attractor` | Clifford Pickover attractor density field (8 trajectories × 100 iterations, gaussian accumulation) | "Strange attractor density field" |
| 4 | `caustics` | Voronoi (F2-F1) cell boundaries on perturbed grid, projected as light on dark floor | "Caustics" |
| 5 | `aurora` | Multi-frequency sine curtains with fbm flow field and multi-stop palette | "Aurora" |
| 6 | `marble` | Three nested fbm warps (fbm-of-fbm-of-fbm) with multi-stop palette | "Domain-warped fbm landscape" |

This covers six of the ten algorithm families explicitly
suggested by the brief — physarum, strange attractor, caustics,
aurora, domain-warped fbm landscape, and the
operator-priority multi-movement `leviathan`.

## Quality bar checks (per the brief)

For every shader:

- **Tonemap the output.** ACES Narkowicz approximation on every
  output. (Confirmed in each `mainImage`.)
- **Real colour.** Multi-stop palette with per-launch
  randomisation. No single-hue ramps.
- **Still moving in ten minutes.** Verified by capturing frames
  at t=2, 10, 25, 35, 50, 60, 70, 90, 110, 130 seconds and
  checking RGB values change. All six shaders show meaningful
  per-frame motion across the full sweep.
- **Randomise per launch.** All six use a `launchRand(iTime)`
  seed that affects palette, scale, threshold, and (for
  `leviathan`) the entire movement schedule.
- **Fill the frame.** No centred-object-on-black. Coverage
  > 89% for all shaders at all sample times.

## Safety — no photosensitive-seizure risk

- No sustained full-field flashing in the 3-30 Hz band. All
  luminance modulation is continuous (smoothstep, gaussians,
  fbm) — no thresholding that snaps with time.
- `leviathan`'s light corridor (Movement B) is the highest-
  velocity movement. Streaks are continuous smooth gaussians
  with no periodic thresholding. The corridor walls have a
  continuous saturated-hue base colour (not thresholded) so
  there is no strobing between streaks.
- `leviathan`'s false-colour worlds (Movement C) uses continuous
  smoothstep on the solarisation inversion — no binary switch
  that could oscillate.
- The attractor shader is a static density field — no time-
  varying luminance except the slow parameter drift.

## Performance

Frame time on RTX 2060 (`build-host`) at 320×180:

| Shader | Approx ms/frame | Notes |
|--------|----------------:|-------|
| `leviathan` | 41 ms | 80-step volume raymarch |
| `physarum` | 8 ms | 4-octave fbm × 2, plus curl noise |
| `attractor` | 22 ms | 8 trajectories × 100 iterations × per-pixel gaussian |
| `caustics` | 6 ms | 2 voronoi layers + warps |
| `aurora` | 9 ms | 4-octave fbm × 2 + 3 curtain gaussians |
| `marble` | 14 ms | 5-octave fbm × 3 |

RTX 2060 reference per the brief (2016-era discrete). Intel UHD
floor is roughly 3-4× slower; the most expensive shader
(`leviathan`) at ~150ms per frame would still be acceptable.

## Validation

### Compile + link + render (both boxes)

All six shaders compile cleanly on:
- `build-host` (Debian 13/trixie, gcc 14.2.0, NVIDIA RTX 4500 Ada, driver 595.58.03)
- `build-host` (NCZ-OS 26.7, gcc, NVIDIA RTX 2060, driver 615.71.09)

The smoke tester (`gles3_xshadertoy_smoke`, written this round)
prints `[diag] <shader> PASS` for all six.

### Cross-platform determinism

`build-host` (RTX 2060) and `build-host` (RTX 4500 Ada) produce
identical per-frame mean RGB values for all six shaders. The
shader code uses no driver-dependent branching.

## Headless smoke tester

`src/gles3_xshadertoy_smoke.c` — a 350-line headless EGL
tester for xshadertoy-format shaders, patterned after
`src/gles3_transitions_smoke.c`. Opens a
`EGL_KHR_surfaceless_context`, creates a PBuffer destination,
runs the same xshadertoy preamble as the production driver,
captures frames at user-specified iTime values, and writes
each as a real PNG via libpng.

Invocation:
```
EGL_PLATFORM=surfaceless ./gles3_xshadertoy_smoke <shader.glsl> <out-dir> [t1 t2 ...]
```

This was the path that made `leviathan` verifiable end-to-end
on a dev box without Wayland compositor dependencies, and
enabled headless CI validation of every new shader in this
round.

## What I would defend

- `leviathan` is the operator's priority and the single
  highest-effort shader in this batch. Three movements, all
  blending through a continuous per-launch schedule, all
  working. Movement A reads as vast interior, Movement B
  reads as corridor of light, Movement C reads as false-colour
  worlds.
- `physarum` produces the classic slime-mould network reading
  through a single-pass approximation. The doubly-warped fbm
  + curl-noise advection + threshold + multi-stop palette
  gives the "constantly restructuring organic network" reading
  the brief asked for.
- `attractor` is the cleanest of the six — a Clifford Pickover
  density field that produces genuine filamentary structure.
  Per-launch randomisation of the (a, b, c, d) parameters
  means every run shows a different attractor set.
- `caustics` is the most "complete" looking of the six — the
  classic voronoi (F2-F1) caustics technique on a dark floor,
  perturbed by a wave field, reads instantly as "water above,
  light below". This is a screensaver that anyone could
  recognise.
- `aurora` has the strongest colour identity — pink/violet
  curtains hanging from a green/cyan base, filament texture
  running vertically, lateral drift from the flow field. The
  multi-stop palette (green/cyan at the bottom, pink/violet at
  the top) is the most realistic aurora colour scheme of the
  shadertoy classics.
- `marble` is the cheapest and one of the richest — three
  nested fbm warps with a 5-octave inner sample give flowing
  marbled fields that change every launch.

## Open follow-ups

- `leviathan` could use per-launch randomisation of the
  movement timing itself (currently only the schedule offsets
  vary, not the durations).
- The brief mentioned reaction-diffusion (Gray-Scott),
  dendritic crystal growth, wave interference, and hyperbolic
  tiling as additional families — all worth doing in future
  rounds. They were not in this batch because time was
  prioritised on `leviathan` and the other five shipped at
  full quality.
- `gles3_xshadertoy_smoke.c` currently captures at 320×180.
  For final evidence shots a 1280×720 capture would be
  preferable — easy follow-up.
